Story 01 · Football Academy

Where young players learn to belong.

Timiza Ndoto Academy is the foundation’s youth football programme. It gives children in different age groups a structured place to train, build friendships and grow through the game.

01

A place to belong

For many children, the first opportunity they need is simply a safe and organised place to take part. The academy brings young players together with a coach, a team identity and the confidence that comes from being included.

Children from different age groups learn the foundations of football while developing discipline, teamwork and belief in their own potential.

02

Learning through movement

Every session begins with the habits that make development possible: preparation, attention and consistency. Guided warm-ups help the players move well, listen to instructions and work together.

The aim is not only to improve football technique. It is to build routines and values the children can carry into school, family life and future opportunities.
