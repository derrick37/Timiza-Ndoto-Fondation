Story 01 · Football Academy

Where young players learn to belong.

Timiza Ndoto Academy gives children in different age groups a structured place to train, make friends and develop the discipline that carries beyond football.

The work

The academy creates access to regular football training for children who may otherwise have few organised opportunities. Players are guided through warm-ups, team sessions and age-appropriate development in a supportive environment.

Alongside football technique, the programme builds confidence, discipline, teamwork and the belief that every child deserves the chance to improve.

  • Training for different age groups.
  • Supportive coaching and team identity.
  • Character development through consistent participation.
