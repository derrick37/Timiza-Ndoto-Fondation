Story 03 · Village Tournaments

A local platform for players to be seen.

Village tournaments bring people together and give local athletes a meaningful place to compete. The foundation helps teams participate with greater dignity and confidence.

The work

By supporting village competitions, Timiza Ndoto Foundation helps local players gain a platform, represent their communities and continue pursuing sport. The support is practical as well as encouraging.

During tournaments the foundation has provided food such as bread to players, while also handing out kits and boots to teams that need equipment.

  • Support for locally organised tournaments.
  • Match-day food for participating players.
  • Kits and boots for community teams.
