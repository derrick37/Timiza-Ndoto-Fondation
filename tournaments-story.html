Story 03 · Village Tournaments

A local platform for players to be seen.

Village tournaments bring people together and give local athletes a meaningful place to compete. The foundation helps players and teams participate with greater dignity and confidence.

01

Support beyond the whistle

Match days can be demanding, especially for players taking part with limited resources. Timiza Ndoto Foundation supports local competitions with practical care, including food such as bread for participating athletes.

Small interventions help players feel valued and allow them to focus on representing their teams and communities.

02

Equipped to take part

The foundation also provides kits and boots to local teams that may not have the basic equipment they need to compete.

By supporting village tournaments in this way, Timiza Ndoto helps create a visible platform for local talent while strengthening pride, participation and community spirit.
