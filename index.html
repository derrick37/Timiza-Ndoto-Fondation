The story behind the work

A football journey that grew into a community mission.

Delvin Muganda experienced how limited coaching and exposure can hold talented young people back. His journey through Kenyan football and later the United States and Austria inspired Timiza Ndoto.

The academy was the beginning—not the whole story. The foundation now uses sport, education and practical support to create opportunity across the wider community.

Serving dreams on and off the field.
