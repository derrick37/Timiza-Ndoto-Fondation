Our story

The engine behind the dream

Built from the opportunity one player wished he had.

01

Late start.
Long journey.
New pathway.

Delvin Muganda wanted to become a professional footballer, but organised sport came late. Proper coaching was limited. Exposure was scarce. Opportunities often depended on being noticed early.

He kept going—reaching the Kenya Premier League and later playing in the United States and Austria. The journey proved what determination can unlock, but also revealed how many young players are left behind before their potential is fully developed.

“Timiza Ndoto exists so the next player does not have to begin with the same disadvantages.”

Through its football academy, the foundation brings together high-level coaching, competitive development and access to clubs and universities, while building discipline, resilience and a never-give-up mentality that lasts far beyond football.
