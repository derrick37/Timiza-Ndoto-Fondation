Our story

The story behind the foundation

Delvin's journey started in football, but the mission grew into community work.

Timiza Ndoto Foundation was born from lived experience. Delvin Muganda knew what it meant to dream big, work hard and still face limited coaching, little exposure and too few opportunities at the right time.

His football journey carried him from Kenya to the United States and Austria. Along the way, he saw both the power of sport and the gaps that hold many young people back. That is why the foundation exists: not only to develop footballers, but to stand with the wider community.

“The academy is one part of the work. The larger dream is to use that same spirit to uplift families, women and local talent across the village.”

Today, Timiza Ndoto Foundation uses football as one doorway to opportunity, while also investing in women empowerment, village education, dignity support and local tournaments that give players a platform to be seen and encouraged.

Timiza Ndoto Academy

The academy is one part of the foundation's wider mission.

At the heart of the academy is the belief that every child deserves a place to learn, play and be guided well.

The academy brings together young players in different age groups, helping them build football fundamentals, teamwork, confidence and character. Coaching sessions are not only about technique — they are also about discipline, commitment and belonging.

  • Structured football training for different age groups.
  • Warm-ups, team sessions and a supportive coaching environment.
  • A pathway that introduces young players to opportunity and belief.

Women empowerment

Dignity, knowledge and practical support for village women.

The foundation's work with women is centred on empowerment that is both educational and practical.

Many village women are the breadwinners in their households. Timiza Ndoto Foundation creates spaces where they can be educated on family planning, sanitary hygiene and money-making ventures that strengthen independence and resilience.

That support is matched with action — including the distribution of mattresses and other essential items to women and families who need them most.

  • Education on family planning and sanitary hygiene.
  • Conversations around income-generating and money-making ventures.
  • Practical aid including mattresses and other essentials.

Village tournaments

Giving local players a platform — and supporting them beyond the whistle.

Timiza Ndoto Foundation also stands behind local village tournaments, because community sport deserves a platform.

By supporting matches and tournaments, the foundation helps create visibility for local talent and encourages players who may otherwise go unnoticed. Support goes beyond applause — it includes practical help such as food, kits and boots that allow athletes and teams to participate with dignity.

  • Giving village players a platform to compete and be seen.
  • Supporting match days with food and encouragement.
  • Providing kits and boots to strengthen local teams.
