Story 02 · Women Empowerment

Knowledge, dignity and practical support.

Many village women are the breadwinners in their homes. The foundation combines useful education with direct assistance that responds to everyday needs.

The work

Community sessions bring women together to discuss family planning, sanitary hygiene and practical money-making ventures. The aim is to give women information they can use to make decisions, protect their wellbeing and strengthen household income.

The foundation also distributes mattresses and other essential items where immediate material support is needed.

  • Family planning and sanitary hygiene education.
  • Ideas for income-generating activities.
  • Mattresses and other household essentials.
